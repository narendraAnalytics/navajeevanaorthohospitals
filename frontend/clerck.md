# Knee Replacement Recovery — What To Do After Knee Surgery

 ## What should I do after knee replacement surgery?

 After knee replacement surgery at Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals, here is what patients typically need to do:

 **First 24–48 hours (in hospital):**
 - Rest with the operated leg slightly elevated to reduce swelling
 - Begin ankle pumping exercises as instructed by the physiotherapist
 - Use ice packs on the knee to control swelling (as directed by nursing staff)
 - Start standing and walking short distances with physiotherapist support

 **First 1–2 weeks (at home):**
 - Keep the wound clean and dry; follow dressing change instructions
 - Do the prescribed physiotherapy exercises daily (knee bending, straightening, leg raises)
 - Walk with a walker or crutches as instructed — do not put full weight until cleared
 - Elevate the leg when sitting or resting to reduce swelling
 - Take prescribed medicines on schedule
 - Attend all outpatient physiotherapy sessions

 **Weeks 3–6:**
 - Gradually increase walking distance with physiotherapist guidance
 - Begin stair climbing exercises when approved by your doctor
 - Continue knee strengthening and range-of-motion exercises
 - Attend follow-up appointment (usually at 2 weeks and 6 weeks after surgery)

 **Things to avoid after knee surgery:**
 - Avoid bending the knee beyond what the surgeon allows in early recovery
 - Do not sit on very low chairs or squat deeply until cleared
 - Avoid driving until the surgeon approves
 - Do not skip physiotherapy sessions — they are essential for full recovery

 **Warning signs — contact hospital immediately:**
 - Sudden increase in knee pain or swelling
 - Redness, warmth, or discharge from the wound
 - Fever above 100°F / 38°C
 - Leg becomes cold, pale, or numb
 - Difficulty breathing or chest pain

 Recovery after knee replacement typically takes 6–12 weeks for most daily activities
 and 3–6 months for full strength. Physiotherapy is the most important part of recovery.
