# Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation

## Restore Movement. Rebuild Strength. Return to Life.

Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation are essential components of orthopedic care that help patients recover from injuries, surgeries, fractures, joint replacements, sports injuries, spine conditions, and chronic musculoskeletal disorders.

Our goal is not only to reduce pain but also to restore mobility, improve strength, enhance function, and help patients return safely to their normal daily activities.

---

# What Is Physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy is a healthcare specialty focused on improving movement, reducing pain, restoring physical function, and preventing future injuries.

It uses evidence-based techniques including:

* Therapeutic exercises
* Strength training
* Stretching programs
* Manual therapy
* Mobility training
* Balance training
* Posture correction
* Functional rehabilitation

Physiotherapy is often recommended after surgery, injury, fracture treatment, or for chronic orthopedic conditions.

---

# What Is Rehabilitation?

Rehabilitation is a structured recovery process designed to help patients regain physical function after:

* Surgery
* Injury
* Fracture
* Joint replacement
* Sports injury
* Spine surgery
* Neurological conditions
* Prolonged illness

The primary goal is to restore independence and improve quality of life.

## Perform Home Exercises

Home exercise programs are an important part of treatment.

## Stay Active

Within recommended limits.

## Maintain Good Nutrition

Proper nutrition supports healing and recovery.

## Stay Hydrated

Hydration helps overall health and recovery.

---

# What NOT To Do During Rehabilitation

## Do Not Skip Exercises

Irregular participation slows recovery.

## Do Not Rush Recovery

Progress should be gradual and safe.

## Avoid Overexertion

Too much activity can delay healing.

## Do Not Ignore Pain

Persistent or worsening pain should be reported.

## Avoid Poor Posture

Incorrect posture may increase stress on joints and muscles.

## Do Not Return to Sports Prematurely

Return-to-sport decisions should be guided by your healthcare team.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## How Long Will Physiotherapy Take?

The duration depends on:

* Your condition
* Severity of injury
* Surgical procedure
* Recovery goals

Some patients require a few weeks, while others may benefit from several months of rehabilitation.

---

## Is Physiotherapy Painful?

Some exercises may cause temporary discomfort, but physiotherapy should not cause severe pain.

Always inform your therapist if pain increases significantly.

---

## Do I Need Physiotherapy After Surgery?

Yes. Physiotherapy is often a critical part of recovery after orthopedic surgery.

---

## Can Physiotherapy Prevent Surgery?

In some cases, physiotherapy may help manage symptoms and improve function, potentially reducing the need for surgery.

---

## Is Home Physiotherapy Effective?

Yes. Home physiotherapy can be highly effective when performed under professional guidance.
