# Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Services — Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals

## Purpose

This document provides general information about physiotherapy and rehabilitation services available at Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals.

Physiotherapy plays an important role in recovery after:

* Orthopedic surgery
* Fractures
* Sports injuries
* Joint replacement
* Back pain
* Neck pain
* Arthritis
* Mobility problems

Recovery programs are customized according to the patient's condition and treatment plan.

---

# What Is Physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy is a rehabilitation service that helps patients:

* Improve movement
* Improve flexibility
* Increase strength
* Reduce stiffness
* Improve balance
* Improve mobility
* Return to daily activities safely

The rehabilitation plan is determined by the physiotherapy team and treating orthopedic doctor.

---

# Home Physiotherapy Services

## Does Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals provide home physiotherapy?

Yes.

Home physiotherapy services may be available for eligible patients when recommended by the treating doctor or physiotherapy team.

This service may be helpful for:

* Elderly patients
* Post-surgery patients
* Patients with mobility limitations
* Patients recovering from fractures
* Patients unable to travel comfortably

Availability depends on:

* Branch location
* Service area coverage
* Physiotherapist availability
* Patient condition

---

## What happens during a home physiotherapy visit?

The physiotherapist may:

* Assess recovery progress
* Guide rehabilitation exercises
* Monitor mobility improvement
* Review walking techniques
* Evaluate safety concerns
* Update exercise plans

---

## Can all patients receive home physiotherapy?

Not always.

Some patients may be advised to attend physiotherapy sessions at the hospital where additional rehabilitation equipment and facilities are available.

The physiotherapy team will recommend the most appropriate option.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## When should physiotherapy begin after surgery?

The timing depends on:

* Type of surgery
* Medical condition
* Surgeon recommendations

Patients should follow the guidance provided by their orthopedic surgeon and physiotherapy team.

---

## How many physiotherapy sessions will I need?

The number of sessions varies depending on:

* Diagnosis
* Surgery type
* Recovery progress
* Rehabilitation goals

The physiotherapy team will create an individualized plan.

---

## Can I perform exercises at home?

Many patients receive home exercise programs.

However:

* Exercises should be performed exactly as instructed
* New exercises should not be started without professional guidance
* Painful exercises should be reported to the physiotherapy team

---

## What should I do if an exercise causes pain?

Stop the exercise and contact the physiotherapy team for guidance.

---

## How long does rehabilitation take?

Recovery timelines vary depending on:

* Age
* Health status
* Injury severity
* Surgery type
* Participation in rehabilitation

Some patients improve within weeks while others may require several months of rehabilitation.
