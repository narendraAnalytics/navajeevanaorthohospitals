# Insurance & Billing — Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals

## Overview

Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals provides assistance for eligible patients using government health schemes and selected private health insurance providers.

Insurance approvals are subject to:

* Patient eligibility
* Scheme guidelines
* Insurance policy terms
* Required documentation
* Medical necessity
* Authorization approval

The hospital cannot guarantee approval for any treatment, surgery, investigation, or hospitalization.

Final approval is determined by the respective scheme authority, insurance provider, and applicable regulations.

---

# Accepted Health Schemes and Insurance

## Dr. YSR Aarogyasri

Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals supports eligible beneficiaries under the Dr. YSR Aarogyasri Scheme.

Coverage is subject to:

* Patient eligibility
* Government guidelines
* Approved treatment packages
* Authorization approval

Patients may be required to provide:

* Aadhaar Card
* Aarogyasri Card
* Referral documents (if applicable)
* Supporting medical records

---

## Ayushman Bharat – PM-JAY

Eligible patients may avail benefits under Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY), subject to applicable government rules and package eligibility. PM-JAY provides cashless hospitalization benefits for eligible families through empanelled hospitals.

Patients may be asked to provide:

* Ayushman Card
* Aadhaar Card
* Identity verification documents
* Relevant medical records

---

## Star Health Insurance

Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals accepts eligible Star Health Insurance policies.

Coverage depends on:

* Policy terms
* Waiting periods
* Exclusions
* Sum insured
* Authorization approval

Patients should contact their insurer for policy-specific coverage details.

---

# Cashless Treatment

## Is cashless treatment available?

Cashless treatment may be available for eligible patients under approved schemes and insurance policies.

Cashless approval is not automatic.

Approval depends on:

* Eligibility verification
* Documentation review
* Medical necessity
* Insurer or scheme authorization

---

## What documents are usually required?

Patients may be required to submit:

* Aadhaar Card
* Government-issued ID
* Insurance card or policy details
* Aarogyasri card
* Ayushman card
* Doctor's recommendation
* Investigation reports
* Hospital admission documents

---

## How long does approval take?

Approval timelines vary depending on:

* Scheme rules
* Insurance provider
* Required documentation
* Treatment category
* Medical urgency

Some approvals may be granted quickly, while others may require additional review.

---

# Emergency Care Policy

## What happens during emergencies?

Emergency medical stabilization is prioritized.

The hospital will not delay urgent medical assessment solely because insurance authorization is pending.

However:

* Insurance approval may require additional processing time
* Additional documents may be requested
* Coverage decisions remain subject to insurer or scheme approval

---

## Who makes the final treatment decision?

The treating orthopedic doctor is responsible for clinical decisions.

Insurance providers do not determine emergency medical necessity.

The treating doctor decides:

* Admission requirements
* Emergency treatment
* Surgery requirements
* Diagnostic investigations
* Discharge readiness

---

# What May Not Be Covered

Coverage depends on scheme rules and policy terms.

Examples that may have restrictions include:

* Non-covered procedures
* Waiting-period conditions
* Excluded treatments
* Non-medical consumables
* Administrative charges
* Upgrades beyond approved room category
* Non-authorized procedures

Patients should verify coverage before planned treatment whenever possible.

---

# Billing and Payments

## Accepted Payment Methods

The hospital may accept:

* Cash
* UPI
* Debit Card
* Credit Card
* Net Banking

Availability may vary by branch.

---

## Can patients request an itemized bill?

Yes.

Patients may request a detailed itemized bill for insurance claims, reimbursement, and personal records.

---

# Important Disclaimer

Insurance acceptance does not guarantee insurance approval.

Coverage decisions depend on:

* Scheme eligibility
* Policy conditions
* Medical necessity
* Required documentation
* Authorization approval

For emergency orthopedic situations, immediate medical care takes priority, while insurance processing may continue in parallel.

Final clinical decisions always remain with the treating doctor.
