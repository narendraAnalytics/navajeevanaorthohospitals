# Appointment FAQ — Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals

## About Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals

Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals is a specialized orthopedic healthcare network with branches in:

* Bhimavaram
* Palakollu
* Eluru
* Vijayawada

The hospital provides orthopedic consultations, fracture care, joint replacement services, sports injury treatment, physiotherapy support, trauma care, diagnostic services, teleconsultation, and emergency orthopedic services.

---

# Hospital Timings

## What are the Orthopedic OPD timings?

Orthopedic Outpatient Department (OPD) timings are:

* Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
* Sunday: Emergency Services Only

## What are the registration timings?

Patient registration is available:

* Monday to Saturday: 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM

## Is emergency service available?

Yes. Emergency orthopedic services are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## Are all branches open on Sundays?

Routine OPD services are not available on Sundays. Only emergency services are available.

# Appointment Booking

## How can I book an appointment?

Appointments can be booked through:

* Hospital website
* Phone call
* Walk-in registration at the hospital

## Can I book an appointment online?

Yes. Patients can use the hospital website to select a branch, doctor, date, and available appointment slot.

## Can I book an appointment by phone?

Yes. Appointment staff can assist with scheduling, rescheduling, and cancellations over the phone.

## Can I walk in without an appointment?

Yes. Walk-in patients are accepted based on doctor availability and patient volume.

## Is prior registration required?

No. New patients can register during appointment booking.

## Can family members book appointments on behalf of patients?

Yes. Family members may schedule appointments for patients.

# Specialist Appointments

## Can I choose a specific orthopedic doctor?

Yes. Patients may select a specific doctor based on availability.

## Can I book directly with a specialist?

Yes. Direct specialist appointments are available.

No. Patients may directly consult orthopedic specialists without a referral.

## Can I request a second opinion?

Yes. Patients may schedule appointments with another specialist for a second opinion.

# New Patient Information

## What information is required for registration?

Patients may be asked to provide:

* Age
* Gender

* Address
* Government-issued identification

Please bring:

* Insurance card (if applicable)
* Previous prescriptions

* X-rays
* MRI scans
* CT scans
* Blood reports
* Surgery records
* Current medication list

Patients are advised to arrive 15–30 minutes before the scheduled appointment time to complete registration and verification procedures.

## What should I wear for an orthopedic consultation?

Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy examination of the affected body part.

Most orthopedic consultations take approximately 15–30 minutes depending on case complexity.

## Why am I experiencing delays?

Appointment schedules may occasionally be affected by:

* Emergency cases
* Surgical emergencies
* Complex consultations
* Unexpected medical situations

## Do emergency patients receive priority?

Yes. Emergency and trauma patients receive immediate medical attention when necessary.

No. Existing patient records remain available within the hospital system.

# Teleconsultation Services

## Does Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals offer teleconsultation?

Yes. Teleconsultation services are available.

## What conditions are suitable for teleconsultation?

Teleconsultation may be suitable for:

* Follow-up reviews
* Physiotherapy guidance
* Report discussions
* Recovery monitoring
* Non-emergency orthopedic concerns

## What conditions require an in-person visit?

Patients should visit the hospital for:

* Fractures
* Severe injuries
* Joint dislocations
* Emergency conditions
* Physical examinations requiring imaging

Yes. Patients may upload or share reports before consultation.

## Can prescriptions be issued after teleconsultation?

Yes, when medically appropriate and permitted under applicable regulations.

# Emergency vs Appointment

## When should I visit the Emergency Department?

Seek immediate emergency care for:

* Severe fractures
* Major accidents
* Heavy bleeding
* Joint dislocations
* Sudden inability to move a limb
* Post-surgical complications
* Severe swelling after injury
* Severe chest pain
* Difficulty breathing

Emergency orthopedic conditions often require immediate medical evaluation.

## When should I book a routine appointment?

Routine appointments are appropriate for:

* Arthritis consultations
* Chronic knee pain
* Back pain
* Follow-up visits
* Sports injury assessment
* Physiotherapy review
* Joint replacement consultations
