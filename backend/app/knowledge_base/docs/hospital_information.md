# Hospital Information — Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals

## About Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals

Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals is a specialized orthopedic healthcare network dedicated to providing comprehensive orthopedic, trauma, rehabilitation, and musculoskeletal care.

The hospital network operates across multiple locations in Andhra Pradesh and provides advanced orthopedic treatment, fracture care, joint replacement services, sports injury management, physiotherapy, diagnostics, and emergency orthopedic care.

# Important Safety Notice

The hospital AI assistant provides general information only.

The AI assistant does not:

* Diagnose diseases
* Interpret MRI reports
* Interpret CT reports
* Interpret X-Rays
* Prescribe medication
* Modify treatment plans

Medical decisions are made only by qualified healthcare professionals.

---

# Emergency Notice

Patients experiencing any of the following should seek immediate medical attention:

* Chest Pain
* Difficulty Breathing
* Severe Bleeding
* Major Trauma
* Suspected Fracture with Deformity
* Sudden Inability to Move a Limb
* Loss of Consciousness

In emergencies, immediate medical care should be prioritized over online consultation.
