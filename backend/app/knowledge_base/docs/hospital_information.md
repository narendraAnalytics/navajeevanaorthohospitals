# Hospital Information — Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals

## About Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals

Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals is a specialized orthopedic healthcare network dedicated to providing comprehensive orthopedic, trauma, rehabilitation, and musculoskeletal care in Andhra Pradesh, India.

The hospital network is headquartered in Bhimavaram, West Godavari District, Andhra Pradesh, and serves patients across the region with branches in Bhimavaram, Palakollu, Eluru, and Vijayawada.

Services offered across branches include: advanced orthopedic treatment, fracture care, joint replacement surgery, sports injury management, physiotherapy and rehabilitation, diagnostic imaging (X-Ray, MRI, CT Scan), and 24/7 emergency orthopedic care.

---

## OPD Timings — Outpatient Department Hours

### What are the OPD timings at Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals?

The Orthopedic OPD (Outpatient Department) is open:

* **Monday to Saturday:** 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
* **Sunday:** Emergency Services Only (OPD is closed on Sundays)

OPD consultation hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on all weekdays and Saturdays. Walk-in patients are welcome during OPD hours. Scheduled appointments are preferred to reduce waiting time.

### What time does the hospital open?

The hospital opens for outpatient services at 9:00 AM Monday through Saturday. Registration and token issuance starts from 8:30 AM.

### Registration Timings

Patient registration counter is open:

* **Monday to Saturday:** 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM

Patients should arrive early for registration to ensure a consultation slot on the same day. OPD registration closes at 5:30 PM even though OPD hours extend to 6:00 PM.

### What are the doctor consultation timings?

Doctors are available for consultation in OPD from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekdays and Saturdays. Specific doctor availability may vary — please book an appointment in advance to confirm your doctor's schedule.

### Are there morning and evening OPD slots?

Yes. OPD runs across both morning and evening:

* Morning session: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
* Evening session: 2:00 PM – 6:00 PM

### Is the hospital open on Sundays?

OPD is closed on Sundays. Only emergency orthopedic services are available 24/7 including Sundays.

### Is the hospital open on public holidays?

Emergency services remain available 24/7 on all public holidays. OPD and scheduled appointment services may be suspended on government-declared holidays. Patients are advised to call ahead on public holidays.

## Emergency Services

### Are emergency services available?

Yes. Emergency orthopedic services are available **24 hours a day, 7 days a week** at all branches.

Emergency services are designed to evaluate and stabilize urgent orthopedic injuries, fractures, trauma cases, and post-surgical emergencies at any time — day or night, weekday or Sunday or holiday.

### What emergencies does the hospital handle?

* Fractures (simple and complex)
* Road Traffic Accident injuries
* Sports trauma and ligament injuries
* Joint Dislocations
* Severe orthopedic pain requiring urgent care
* Post-surgical emergencies and wound complications
* Falls and crush injuries

For life-threatening emergencies, call emergency services or go to the nearest emergency room immediately. Do not wait for an online consultation.

## Appointment Booking

### How to book an appointment at Navajeevana Ortho Hospitals?

Appointments can be booked through:

* **Online booking:** Via the official hospital website or patient portal
* **Walk-in registration:** Visit the hospital registration counter (8:30 AM – 5:30 PM, Monday–Saturday)
* **Appointment helpline:** Call the hospital appointment desk

Online booking is recommended to avoid long waiting times and to secure a slot with your preferred doctor.

### Can I walk in without an appointment?

Yes, walk-in patients are accepted during OPD hours (9:00 AM – 6:00 PM, Monday–Saturday). However, wait times may be longer without a prior appointment. Booking an appointment in advance is strongly recommended.

### How early should I arrive before my appointment?

Patients should arrive at least 15–20 minutes before their scheduled appointment time to complete registration formalities.

## Teleconsultation Services

### Is teleconsultation available?

Yes. Teleconsultation is available for:

* Follow-up consultations after a hospital visit
* Report and scan result discussions
* Recovery monitoring after surgery
* Physiotherapy guidance
* Non-emergency orthopedic questions

Teleconsultation is **not** recommended for:

* Suspected fractures or dislocations
* Severe or acute injuries
* Any condition requiring physical examination
* Emergency orthopedic situations

## Orthopedic Departments

### General Orthopedics

Treats:
* Joint pain (knee, hip, shoulder, elbow, wrist, ankle)
* Arthritis (osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis)
* Back pain and neck pain
* Muscle and tendon conditions
* Mobility and gait disorders

### Joint Replacement Center

Specializes in:
* Knee Replacement Surgery (Total and Partial)
* Hip Replacement Surgery
* Shoulder Replacement
* Revision Joint Replacement Surgery

Suitable for patients with severe arthritis, joint degeneration, or previous joint surgery requiring revision.

### Sports Medicine

Treats:
* ACL (Anterior Cruciate Ligament) injuries
* Meniscus tears
* Shoulder injuries and rotator cuff conditions
* Ankle sprains and ligament injuries
* Athletic rehabilitation and return-to-sport programs

### Spine Care

Manages:
* Chronic back pain and lower back pain
* Neck pain and cervical spondylosis
* Disc prolapse and herniated discs
* Degenerative spine conditions
* Sciatica and nerve compression

### Trauma and Fracture Care

Treats:
* All types of fractures (simple, compound, stress fractures)
* Accident and road traffic injuries
* Complex trauma with multiple bone injuries
* Emergency orthopedic stabilization

### Pediatric Orthopedics

Treats children and adolescents for:
* Childhood fractures and growth plate injuries
* Bone growth disorders
* Congenital bone and joint conditions
* Clubfoot and limb deformities

### Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation

Services:
* Post-surgery rehabilitation programs
* Joint mobility restoration
* Strength and conditioning after injury
* Gait training and balance therapy
* Sports rehabilitation

## Important Safety Notice

The hospital AI assistant provides general information only.

The AI assistant does not:
* Diagnose diseases or medical conditions
* Interpret MRI, CT, or X-Ray reports
* Prescribe or recommend medications
* Modify existing treatment plans
* Replace in-person doctor consultation

All medical decisions are made only by qualified healthcare professionals at the hospital.

---

## Emergency Notice

Patients experiencing any of the following must seek **immediate in-person medical attention** and should not rely on online consultation:

* Suspected fracture with visible deformity
* Severe uncontrolled pain
* Major trauma or accident injuries
* Sudden inability to move a limb
* Severe swelling after an injury
* Open wounds or bone visible through skin
* Chest pain or difficulty breathing
* Loss of consciousness

In emergencies, call for help or go to the nearest emergency room immediately.
