# Knee Replacement Surgery

## Regain Mobility. Live Pain-Free.

Knee Replacement Surgery, also known as **Total Knee Arthroplasty (TKA)**, is a highly successful orthopedic procedure designed to relieve chronic knee pain, improve mobility, and restore quality of life. It is commonly recommended for patients suffering from severe arthritis, joint damage, or long-term knee degeneration that no longer responds to non-surgical treatments.

---

## What Is Knee Replacement Surgery?

The knee joint is formed by the femur (thigh bone), tibia (shin bone), and patella (kneecap). Over time, the protective cartilage that cushions these bones can wear away due to arthritis, injury, or age-related degeneration.

During knee replacement surgery, the damaged portions of the joint are removed and replaced with specially designed artificial implants made from medical-grade metal and durable plastic. These implants help restore smooth movement and reduce pain.

---

## When Is Knee Replacement Recommended?

Your orthopedic specialist may recommend knee replacement if you experience:

* Persistent knee pain during daily activities
* Difficulty walking, climbing stairs, or standing for long periods
* Severe stiffness or reduced range of motion
* Knee deformity or misalignment
* Pain that continues even while resting
* Limited relief from medications, injections, or physiotherapy

If knee pain significantly affects your quality of life and conservative treatments have failed, knee replacement may be the most effective solution.

---

## Conditions Treated

Knee replacement surgery is commonly performed for:

* Osteoarthritis
* Rheumatoid Arthritis
* Post-Traumatic Arthritis
* Degenerative Joint Disease
* Severe Cartilage Damage
* Advanced Knee Deformities

---

## Types of Knee Replacement

### Total Knee Replacement (TKR)

The entire knee joint surface is replaced with artificial components. This is the most commonly performed knee replacement procedure.

### Partial Knee Replacement (PKR)

Only the damaged section of the knee is replaced while preserving healthy bone and tissue. This option may be suitable for selected patients with localized arthritis.

---

## Benefits of Knee Replacement

Patients often experience:

* Significant reduction in pain
* Improved joint function
* Better mobility and flexibility
* Increased independence
* Enhanced quality of life
* Long-lasting results

Modern knee implants can often last 15–20 years or longer with proper care and follow-up.

---

## Preparing for Surgery

Before surgery, patients typically undergo:

* Orthopedic consultation and examination
* Blood tests and routine investigations
* ECG and medical fitness assessment
* X-rays or advanced imaging studies
* Medication review
* Pre-operative counseling
* Physiotherapy guidance

Patients will receive detailed instructions regarding fasting, medications, and hospital admission procedures.

---

## What Happens During Surgery?

1. Anesthesia is administered.
2. A surgical incision is made over the knee.
3. Damaged cartilage and bone are removed.
4. Artificial implants are positioned and secured.
5. Knee alignment and movement are checked.
6. The incision is closed and dressed.

The procedure usually takes between **1 to 2 hours**, depending on the complexity of the case.

---

## Recovery After Surgery

### Hospital Stay

Most patients begin standing and walking with assistance within 24 hours after surgery. Hospital stay generally ranges from 2 to 5 days depending on recovery progress.

### Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation

Physiotherapy is essential for successful recovery and helps:

* Improve knee flexibility
* Restore muscle strength
* Improve walking ability
* Prevent stiffness
* Accelerate recovery

Our rehabilitation team develops a personalized recovery program for each patient.

### Home Recovery

For a smooth recovery:

* Perform prescribed exercises regularly
* Use walking aids as advised
* Apply ice packs to reduce swelling
* Keep the surgical site clean and dry
* Attend scheduled follow-up visits

Most patients experience significant improvement within a few months following surgery.

---

## Possible Risks and Complications

As with any surgical procedure, knee replacement carries some risks, including:

* Infection
* Blood clots
* Bleeding
* Implant loosening
* Nerve or blood vessel injury
* Joint stiffness

Our orthopedic team follows strict safety protocols to minimize complications and ensure the best possible outcomes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does a knee replacement last?

Most modern knee replacements last between **15 and 20 years**, and often longer with proper care.

### Will I be able to walk normally again?

Yes. Most patients experience substantial improvements in walking, mobility, and overall function after recovery.

### Is knee replacement surgery painful?

Pain is carefully managed with modern anesthesia techniques and post-operative medications. Most patients report significant pain relief once recovery is complete.

### When can I return to normal activities?

Many patients return to routine daily activities within a few weeks, while full recovery may continue for several months.

### Will I need physiotherapy after surgery?

Yes. Physiotherapy plays a crucial role in achieving the best possible outcome and restoring full knee function.
