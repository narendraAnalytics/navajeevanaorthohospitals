Yes. Before writing any code, you should prepare the knowledge base documents first. For your project, **`appointment_faq.md` should be one of the first files created** because appointment-related questions will likely be the most common patient queries. 

For the Orthopedic Hospital AI Agent, I recommend the following structure for **appointment_faq.md**.

# Sections to Include

## 1. Hospital OPD Information

Store information such as:

* Orthopedic OPD timings
* Working days
* Registration timings
* Emergency service availability
* Appointment helpline number
* Online appointment availability
* Walk-in availability

These are among the most common patient questions. ([virrd.com][1])

---

## 2. Appointment Booking FAQ

Examples:

### How can I book an appointment?

* Online portal
* Phone booking
* Reception desk
* Walk-in booking

### Can I book an appointment with a specialist directly?

### Is referral required?

### Can I choose a specific doctor?

### Can family members book on behalf of the patient?

These are common appointment-related questions patients ask. ([Apollo Hospitals][2])

---

## 3. Appointment Rescheduling

Questions such as:

* How do I reschedule?
* How much notice is required?
* Can I change doctors?
* Can I change appointment dates?

---

## 4. Appointment Cancellation

Questions such as:

* How do I cancel?
* Is there a cancellation fee?
* What happens if I miss my appointment?

---

## 5. Walk-In Patients

Include:

### Are walk-ins accepted?

### Will I need to wait longer?

### Are specialists available for walk-ins?

---

## 6. First-Time Patient Guide

Very important.

Include:

### What documents should I bring?

* Aadhaar card
* Insurance card
* Previous prescriptions
* Previous surgery records
* X-rays
* MRI scans
* Blood reports

### How early should I arrive?

Hospitals commonly recommend arriving before the scheduled appointment. ([Vivekananda Hospital][3])

---

## 7. Follow-Up Appointments

Questions such as:

### How do I book a follow-up?

### Do I need to register again?

### Can follow-up visits be done online?

---

## 8. Orthopedic-Specific Questions

This is where your hospital becomes different from a generic hospital.

Examples:

### When should I see an orthopedic doctor?

### Which conditions are treated?

* Knee pain
* Hip pain
* Arthritis
* Fractures
* Sports injuries
* Back pain
* Joint replacement follow-up

These are common orthopedic concerns. ([Dr Kunal Aneja][4])

---

## 9. Doctor Availability

Examples:

### How can I know if my doctor is available?

### Can I request a different orthopedic specialist?

### What if my doctor is unavailable?

---

## 10. Teleconsultation FAQ

Examples:

### Can I consult online?

### Can I show reports during a video consultation?

### Can prescriptions be issued after teleconsultation?

---

## 11. Insurance During Appointment

Basic appointment-level questions only:

### Can I use insurance during OPD consultation?

### Which insurance cards should I bring?

### Does cashless apply to consultations?

(Detailed insurance information belongs in `insurance_billing.md`.)

---

## 12. Emergency vs Appointment

Very important for routing.

Include questions like:

### Should I book an appointment or visit emergency?

Examples:

Emergency:

* Severe fracture
* Heavy bleeding
* Unable to move limb
* Severe accident injury

Appointment:

* Chronic knee pain
* Arthritis consultation
* Follow-up visit
* Physiotherapy review

This information will help the AI classify tickets correctly later.

---

## 13. Contact Information

Store:

* Appointment number
* Reception number
* Email
* Hospital address
* Emergency number

---

## Recommended Size

For RAG quality:

```text
appointment_faq.md
├─ Hospital Information
├─ Appointment Booking
├─ Appointment Changes
├─ Walk-ins
├─ First Visit
├─ Follow-ups
├─ Orthopedic FAQ
├─ Teleconsultation
├─ Emergency vs Appointment
└─ Contact Information
```

Target:

```text
80–120 FAQ entries
```

This is enough to generate several hundred Chroma chunks and provide strong retrieval quality for appointment-related tickets.
